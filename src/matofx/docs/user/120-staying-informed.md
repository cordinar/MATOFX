# Staying informed

MATOFX SPDM™ keeps you up to date in a few quiet ways.

## Email notifications and what their links do

If you've turned on job emails (on your **account page**), MATOFX emails you when
one of your jobs **starts**, **finishes**, or **fails** — for tools whose author
enabled emails. Not every tool does: emails are **off** on a new tool until an
administrator switches them on for it, so a quiet tool is normal rather than a
sign something is wrong. Each email includes a **link straight back to the job**,
so you can go from your inbox to the result in one click. (If you're signed out,
the link takes you via the sign-in page and then on to the job.)

## Site notices and maintenance banners

From time to time your administrator may post a **notice** — for example, a
warning that the site will be unavailable for maintenance at a certain time. These
appear as a **banner** across the top of the page so you don't miss them.

## Your organisation's tutorials

Your administrator can upload **tutorials** — documents and videos about your
organisation's own tools and workflows. General tutorials appear in the
**Tutorials** card on your home page, and tool- or workflow-specific ones appear
next to that tool or workflow in the Quick Access lists. These are separate from
this built-in documentation: tutorials are about *your* tools, while this guide is
about MATOFX itself.
