# Getting started

## What MATOFX SPDM™ does for you

MATOFX SPDM™ runs your engineering simulation tools for you and keeps the results
organised. You fill in a short form to say what you want a tool to do, MATOFX
queues the work and runs it, and the result is saved alongside the inputs that
produced it — so you (and the colleagues you share with) can find it, read it,
and compare it later.

## Signing in and out

Sign in with the username and password your administrator gave you. To sign out,
use the **Logout** control at the **bottom of the left-hand sidebar** (next to
your name), or the **My Account** tile on your home page. For your safety, MATOFX
also signs you out automatically after a period with no activity (see below).

## The main screen explained

Every page shares the same layout:

- **The sidebar** (on the left) — your sections of the software, grouped into
  **Dashboard**, **Studies**, **Libraries** and **Governance**, with the
  administrator-only **Admin** group pinned to the bottom. The page you're on is
  **highlighted**, and its group opens automatically. At the **top** sits your
  site's name and logo with a **pin** button; at the **bottom** sit your **name**
  and avatar (select either to open your account settings) and the **Logout**
  control.
  - **Pin or unpin** the sidebar with the pin button. Pinned, it stays open.
    Unpinned, it shrinks to a slim strip of icons that **expands when you hover**
    over it — handy for giving the page more room.
  - **Resize** a pinned sidebar by dragging its right-hand edge.
  - Your pin, width and which groups are open are **remembered for you** — they
    follow you to any device you sign in from.
- **The header bar** (across the top) — the **global search** box, a **light/dark
  theme** toggle, a clock in your time zone, and the **help button** (the question
  mark).
- **The main area** — the page you're on. After signing in, this is your home
  page.

## Finding things fast — global search

The **search box** at the top of every page is a quick way to jump to anything
you can see. Start typing and matching **projects, jobs, parametric studies and
optimisations** appear in a drop-down, grouped by type, each showing its status.
Select a result to go straight to it, or press **Enter** to open the first match.
Use the **in:** drop-down beside the box to limit the search to one kind of item.
Search only ever shows things you already have permission to see.

> Global search is for *jumping to* an item by name. To filter completed runs by
> the **input values** they used, use the dedicated **Job Search** page instead
> (the **Job Search** tile on your home page).

## Your home page at a glance

Your home page is the landing screen after you sign in. From the top:

- **A greeting and a summary line** telling you what's happening right now — how
  many jobs are active, how many optimisations or studies are running, and (if
  you're an approver) how many approvals are waiting. Each part only appears when
  there's something to report.
- **Action tiles** — quick starting points:
  - **Create** opens a menu to start a **Project**, a **Scenario**, a **Job**, or
    a **Custom Workflow** — each opens its form right there. (A scenario always
    lives inside a project, so that form asks **which project** first.)
  - **Studies** opens a menu to go to Compare Jobs, Job Cohorts, Parametric
    Studies, Optimisation or Surrogate Models (only the ones switched on appear).
  - **Job Search** opens the page for finding past runs by their inputs.
  - **My Account** opens **My Account Settings** or signs you out.
- **Quick Access** — your **favourite** tools and workflows, each with a
  **Launch** button (choose *in an existing project* or *in a new project*) and,
  where one exists, a **Tutorials** button. Use the **Tools / Workflows / All**
  switch to filter the list; your choice is remembered.
- **Approvals** (approvers only) — a short list of items waiting on your decision,
  each with a **Review** button into the approvals queue.
- **My Work** — four tables, newest first: **My Projects** (with the owner and
  your role on each), **My Jobs**, and — when switched on — **Parametric Studies**
  and **Optimisations**. Select any row to open it, or a column heading to
  **sort**. Each table's **New** button starts a fresh item, and the footer link
  opens the full list.

## Starring favourites

Quick Access shows the tools and workflows you've **starred**. Add a favourite by
selecting the **star** next to a tool or workflow wherever you launch it; remove
one by selecting the filled star on its Quick Access row. (Favourites need to be
switched on by your administrator.)

## Your projects and scenarios

Your work is organised into **projects** and **scenarios**, and you share it with
colleagues at the project level. The **My Projects** table on your home page and
the **Projects** entry in the sidebar are your way in. For how the pieces fit
together — the project → scenario → job hierarchy, creating and archiving
projects, and the Viewer / Member / Owner sharing roles — see *Organising your
work*.

## Automatic sign-out after inactivity

If you leave MATOFX open but don't use it for a while, MATOFX signs you out and
returns you to the sign-in page. This protects your work if you step away from
an unlocked screen. Your administrator sets how long the quiet period is.

You always land on the sign-in page itself — never on a page that has quietly
stopped working — and a short note at the top of the sign-in card tells you why
you're there. Sign in again and MATOFX takes you straight back to the page you
were on. The same thing happens if you come back to a tab you left open
overnight, or reopen one you'd closed: the first thing MATOFX does is check
whether your session is still good, and if it isn't, it sends you to sign in
before you start typing anything you'd lose.

## If you're told all places are in use

Some sites are licensed for a set number of people signed in **at the same
time**. If everyone's places are taken when you try to sign in, MATOFX says so
and asks you to try again shortly — there is nothing wrong with your account or
your password.

Places free up when people sign out, and automatically after a period of
inactivity. If you need in urgently, your administrator can free one for you.

## Getting help inside MATOFX SPDM™

The **help button** (the question mark in the header) offers two destinations:

- **Help Documentation** — opens this guide from anywhere. The **User guide**
  covers everything in this section of the menu.
- **Tutorials** — opens the **Tutorials** page, a single place listing every
  walkthrough and recording your administrators have uploaded, grouped into
  general ones and those tied to a particular tool or workflow. (Tool- and
  workflow-specific tutorials also appear on their Quick Access rows.)
