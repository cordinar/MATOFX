# My Account Settings

**My Account Settings** is where you see your own details and change your
personal settings. It doesn't affect anyone else, and most of it is private to
you.

## Opening My Account Settings

You can reach **My Account Settings** from anywhere in MATOFX SPDM™: select your name
(or initials) at the bottom of the left-hand sidebar, or open the **My Account**
tile on the home page and choose **My Account Settings**.

The page is a stack of cards. Each card is explained below, in the order you'll
see them.

## Your identity

The **Identity** card shows the basics of your account:

- **Username** — the name you sign in with.
- **Email** — where MATOFX sends you notifications.
- **Full name** — your name as shown to colleagues.
- **Role** — your account type (for example *Regular User*).
- **Member since** and **Last sign-in** — useful dates.
- **User number** — a unique number for your account, handy if you ever need to
  quote it to your administrator.

These details are **shown here for reference only** — you can't edit them on this
page. To change your name or email, ask your administrator.

## Your approver status

The **Approver** row tells you whether you're allowed to **approve** other
people's simulations before they run. If you are, MATOFX lists the exact tools
you're responsible for signing off. If you're not, it simply says so. Most users
are not approvers — this is an extra responsibility an administrator grants.

## Your teams

The **Teams** card lists the teams you belong to. Teams are how an administrator
groups people to share access to tools and work. You can't change your own team
membership here — your administrator manages it.

## Region and time

The **Region & time** card has two settings:

- **Language / region** — controls how dates and numbers are written.
- **Time zone** — controls the clock shown at the top of every page.

Each one has a **"Use my browser's setting"** option at the top. Pick that and
MATOFX simply follows whatever your device is set to — handy if you travel.

## Notification preferences

The **Notification preferences** card has a single switch: **Send me job-state
emails**. When it's on, MATOFX emails you when one of your jobs **starts**,
**finishes**, or **fails**.

One thing to know: this only covers tools whose author chose to send emails. If
you've turned the switch on but aren't getting emails for a particular tool, that
tool simply doesn't send them — turning your switch off, though, silences job
emails for **every** tool.

## Result summaries

If your administrator has turned on **AI-assisted result summaries**, a **Result
summaries** card appears with a single switch: **Use AI-enriched result
summaries**. When it's on, the short summary at the top of your job and study
results is rewritten for readability by the configured AI service — always from
the same recorded figures, so nothing is invented. Switch it off to always see
the plain, machine-written summary instead. If the card isn't shown, the feature
isn't enabled on your installation and every summary is the plain version.

## Changing your password

The **Change password** card lets you set a new password:

1. Type your **current** password.
2. Type your **new** password. A checklist shows each rule and ticks it off as
   your password meets it.
3. **Re-type** the new password. A note confirms when the two entries match.

The **Update password** button stays switched off until your current password is
filled in, every rule passes, and the two new entries match. The rules come from
your site's password policy, which your administrator sets.
