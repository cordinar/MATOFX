# A quick tour of the screen

Once you're signed in, every page shares the same layout.

## The top-right controls

In the **top-right corner** of every page sit two small buttons: a **light/dark
theme** toggle, and a **help button** (a question mark) that opens this
documentation — or the tutorials — at any time. The **search box** for jumping
to anything you can see isn't up here — it lives on your **home page**, just
below the greeting (see *Where your work appears*).

## The sidebar

The menu down the left groups everything you can do into sections you can open
and close:

- **Dashboard** — your day-to-day work: your home page, your projects, your
  jobs, and custom workflows.
- **Studies** — comparing jobs and the study tools your site has switched on.
- **Libraries** — shared files and materials.
- **Governance** — approvals and record-keeping.

At the top of the sidebar is a **pin** button: pinned, the menu stays open;
unpinned, it shrinks to icons that expand when you hover. A **Help** group
(Help Documentation and Tutorials) sits near the bottom, and below it are your
**name** (select it for your account settings) and **Logout**. If you're an
administrator you'll also see an **Admin** group above your name. Some entries
only appear when your site has switched a feature on, so your menu may be shorter
or longer than a colleague's.

## Where your work appears

The middle of the screen is the **home page**. It greets you with a summary of
what's running, puts the **search box** right under the greeting, offers quick
buttons to start new work, lists your favourite tools, and shows tables of your
recent projects and jobs. The rest of this documentation walks through each part
in turn.
