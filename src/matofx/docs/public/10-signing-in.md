# Signing in

## Signing in for the first time

Your administrator creates your account and gives you a **username**. The first
time you sign in, use the username and the starting password they gave you. Type
them into the **Sign in** box and select **Sign in** (or press Enter).

Tick **Remember username** to have MATOFX SPDM™ fill your username in automatically the
next time you sign in on the same browser. It only ever remembers the username —
never your password. Leave it unticked on a shared or public computer.

If **Caps Lock** is on while you're typing your password, MATOFX shows a small
**"Caps Lock is on"** reminder beneath the password box — a common cause of a
password being rejected.

## Setting and changing your password

You can change your password at any time once you're signed in, from your
account page (**My Account → My Account Settings → Change password**).

When you choose a new password, MATOFX checks it against your site's password
rules as you type and shows a small checklist. The **Update password** button
stays switched off until every rule is met and your two new-password entries
match. The exact rules are set by your administrator, so they may differ from
one MATOFX site to another.

## If you can't sign in

- **"That username or password isn't right."** — Check for typos and that Caps
  Lock is off (the sign-in box warns you when it is). If you've forgotten your
  password, select **Forgot password?** on the sign-in screen. MATOFX has no
  self-service reset — the link explains that your administrator resets your
  password for you and gives you a new starting password to sign in with.
- **"Your account is deactivated."** — Your administrator has switched your
  account off. Contact them to have it switched back on.
- **"Too many sign-in attempts from your network."** — MATOFX has temporarily
  paused sign-ins from your location after several failed tries (a protection
  against password guessing). Wait a few minutes and try again, or ask your
  administrator if it persists.

If you're still stuck, please contact your administrator.
