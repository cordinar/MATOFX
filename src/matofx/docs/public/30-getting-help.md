# Where to get more help

There are three places to turn when you need a hand.

## The help button inside MATOFX SPDM™

The **question-mark button** in the top bar opens this documentation from
anywhere in MATOFX SPDM™. There is a full **User guide** here covering every part of
the software you'll use day to day. You can also **download this Getting Started
guide** as a single file — use the **Download guide** button at the top of the
page — to keep an offline copy or print it.

## Your administrator

Your administrator looks after your MATOFX site — accounts, the tools available
to you, and which features are switched on. They're the right first contact for
anything to do with **access** ("I can't see a tool", "please reset my
password", "add me to a project").

## The support contact

If your administrator has set one, a **support contact** appears at the bottom
of every page once you're signed in. Use it for problems your administrator
can't resolve.
